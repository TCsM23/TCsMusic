Studio

A small music workstation. Add tracks (instruments, drums, bass, guitar, a sampler, or your own voice), write notes on the piano roll, balance everything on the mixer and download your loop as a WAV, or every track on its own as stems. Every sound is made live in your browser, and your work is remembered here. Tick Sound-effect mode to make short sounds: slide and fade notes in the Piano roll, then export one trimmed take.
